1. Returns within 14 days

You can ask to return an item within 14 days of receiving it. To be accepted, the item must be unused, in the same condition you received it, in its original packaging, with proof of purchase (your order number).

2. Items that can't be returned

For hygiene and safety, these can't be returned once delivered, unless they arrive damaged, defective or wrong:

  • Ready-to-smoke tobacco bowls and all tobacco products
  • Opened consumables such as coal, foil and OKA stems
  • Hoses and bowls that have been used

3. Damaged, defective or wrong items

Please check your order after you receive it. If an item is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will replace it, repair it or refund it, and we cover the shipping for the exchange.

4. OKA performance guarantee

OKA hookahs come with a two-week performance guarantee from the day you receive them. If your OKA hookah doesn't perform as described, contact us within those two weeks and we will help you set it up, replace the faulty part, or refund the hookah.

5. How to return an item

  1. Contact us first at 0105 093 1690 or oka-egypt@hotmail.com with your order number. Items sent back without a return request are not accepted.
  2. Once your request is approved, send the item to: OKA Return Office, Syouf Tram Station, Alexandria — 0114 924 2133.
  3. For change-of-mind returns you pay the return shipping and may choose any courier. For damaged, defective or wrong items, we arrange and pay for it.

6. Refunds

We inspect returned items when they arrive and tell you whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are paid within 10 working days. Orders paid by card are refunded to the same card. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer, InstaPay or mobile wallet — we will ask you for the details. The original shipping fee is refunded only when the item was damaged, defective or wrong.

7. Exchanges

The quickest way to swap an item is to return it as above and place a new order for the item you want. For damaged, defective or wrong items we will send the replacement directly.

8. Orders you refused at delivery

An order refused at the door is not a return. Refused deliveries are covered by section 6 of our Shipping Policy.

9. Contact

For any question about returns or refunds: 0105 093 1690 · oka-egypt@hotmail.com.
