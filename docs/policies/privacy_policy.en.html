This policy explains how OKA Hookahs LLC ("OKA", "we") collects and uses your personal data when you use okaegypt.com, order from us or contact us, in line with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, delivery address, email (if you give one), and messages you send us.
  • Order details: what you ordered, prices, payment method, delivery and return history, and loyalty points.
  • Account details: if you sign in (with a one-time code, Shop, Google or Facebook), the name and email shared with us by that service.
  • Calls: order-confirmation and support calls may be logged and recorded to confirm what was agreed and to improve our service.
  • Device and usage data: pages viewed, cart activity, device and browser type, collected through cookies and similar technology.

2. Why we use it

  • To confirm, deliver and support your orders, including sharing your delivery details with the courier and letting you track your parcel.
  • To handle returns, refunds and your loyalty points.
  • To prevent fraud and false orders, and to recover unpaid shipping costs from refused deliveries as set out in our Shipping Policy.
  • To send you offers by SMS, WhatsApp or email — only if you agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs:

  • Couriers (J&T Express, Bosta): your name, phone, address and the amount to collect.
  • Service providers who run our store and communications, such as Shopify (our e-commerce platform), payment providers and our call and messaging tools.
  • Debt recovery: if a refused delivery leaves an unpaid shipping cost, the details needed to recover it may be shared with a collection agency or the courier.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax purposes, and other data only as long as it's needed for the purposes above. Records of refused deliveries are kept until the related debt is settled.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted (where we're not required to keep it), or withdraw consent to marketing. Contact us using the details below; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Centre.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart and language, keep you signed in, and measure how the site is used. You can block cookies in your browser, but parts of the site (such as the cart) may stop working.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private.

8. Age

Our store is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
