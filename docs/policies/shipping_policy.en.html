2. Order confirmation

Every order is confirmed by a phone call before it is handed to the courier. Please give a correct phone number that you will answer. Orders we cannot confirm may be delayed or cancelled.

3. Payment

You can pay cash on delivery. We may ask for the shipping fee, or the full order amount, in advance in the cases listed in section 6. If an advance payment we asked for is not received, the order is cancelled.

4. Receiving your order (no opening before payment)

Couriers are instructed not to allow a package to be opened or inspected before it is paid for and handed over. Please pay and receive the package first. If anything is wrong with your items after you open them, contact us and we will put it right under our Returns & Refunds Policy.

5. Tracking

Once your order ships you can follow it on the Orders page of our website, including the courier's name and phone number when your parcel is out for delivery.

6. Refused deliveries

When you confirm an order by phone, we and our courier commit real costs to deliver it. If you refuse to receive your order, or refuse to pay its shipping fee, when the courier arrives — after confirming it — the following applies:

  • The shipping cost becomes a debt on your account. This covers the cost of delivering the order to you and returning it to us.
  • OKA may settle this debt as it sees fit, including by adding it to your next order, requiring payment in advance for future orders, and sharing the details needed to recover it with a collection agency or with the courier.
  • Your name and contact details are added to OKA's internal list of refused deliveries. Future orders from you may be refused, or accepted only with payment in advance.

We may also ask for payment in advance for orders to areas with a high rate of refused deliveries.

7. Delays and wrong addresses

Delivery times are estimates. Weather, holidays or courier delays can occasionally add time; we will keep you informed. If the address or phone number you gave is wrong or incomplete and the courier cannot deliver, the order is treated as a refused delivery unless you contact us to correct it before it is returned.
