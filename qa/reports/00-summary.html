OKA App Theme (test) — QA results

Nine testers audited the unpublished test theme: security, money, store data, code, shopping journeys, layout and accessibility, AR and camera, and abuse testing. Everything ran read-only against the live store and against a local copy of the theme rendered with your real products, collections and discounts.

Do not publish this theme yet.

Two blockers expose customer data today, even while the theme is unpublished: its settings hand an API key to every visitor of the preview. Fix the blockers and the critical shipping-fee bug before launch. Most other findings are medium-sized polish.

Blockers and critical

Blocker

The API key reaches every visitor

layout/theme.liquid writes the orders and J&T key into window.OKA. Confirmed in raw HTML on 40 of 40 theme pages for a guest, including 404 and /ar/ pages; only the password page is clean.

SEC-01 · CH-01 · checked by hand
Blocker

That key looks up any customer by phone number

It unlocks the connector's track_by_phone tool, which returns a customer's shipments and Shopify order contents. A script on a guest page read the key and called it (against a local mock only).

SEC-02 · CH-01
Critical

The same key may create and cancel shipments

Orders and tracking share one URL and key. Unless ALLOWED_TOOLS is restricted on the live service, all 19 connector tools are open, including create/cancel shipments, shipment finance and a name-and-phone report.

SEC-03 · needs owner check
Critical

Guests are quoted the Cairo delivery fee everywhere

The cart shows 60 (36 over 300) for all governorates; Shopify charges 70/46 in the delta and 80/56 in the far zone. 72 of 87 cases wrong, no "estimate" label, and the cart's Checkout skips the review page that corrects it.

A shared phone shows the previous guest's order

Name, phone, address and items stay in localStorage with no expiry and appear with Edit and Cancel buttons for the next visitor. Sign-out doesn't clear it.

Scrolling away and back, or switching tabs, while the permission prompt is open starts a second stream that is never stopped.

AR-01 · AR-02 · F-03
High

The keyboard rotates the shop sideways

The portrait lock reads the viewport shape, so an open keyboard in portrait counts as landscape. Reproduced at 360×300 and on landscape tablets. Likely worst in Facebook/Instagram/TikTok browsers.

F-01 · V-19
High

Saved addresses won't work with new customer accounts

The store uses new customer accounts; the theme posts addresses to the classic /account/addresses, and its classic login/register templates are never used.

Hookahs, hoses, coal and bowls are dropped because their products already appear under Premium or Parts. Search chips still show all ten. Confirmed in the browser.

Checkout blocked between 299.01 and 299.99

Shopify's under-300 rate stops at 299.00 in four zones, so no shipping method exists there. Not reachable with today's whole-pound prices. Fix in Shopify shipping settings.

What works

  • For a recognised address, the theme's zone table and its 60/36, 70/46, 80/56 fees match Shopify's delivery zones exactly, including at 300.
  • All 10 collections exist and are published; all 5 bundle variants are purchasable; all 42 images and videos resolve.
  • No horizontal overflow at any of 17 screen sizes in Arabic or English; numbers, phones and prices order correctly in RTL.
  • Collections and search passed 152 of 152 browser checks, including Arabic search.
  • The order service is solid: signed app-proxy requests, replay protection, ownership checks; 66/66 tests pass. No secrets in either repo's git history.
  • A 15-minute, 1,270-page session showed no memory leaks and no errors. 3D loads only when used.
  • Static checks: JavaScript parses, lint 0 errors, all templates and schemas valid.
